Privacy Notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026

ShopSphere is a campus online marketplace run by ARKTECH COMPANY (AAMUSTED, Tanoso, Kumasi) where students buy and sell items. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. We aim to handle your information in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

  • When you create an account: your full name, WhatsApp number, call-line number, an optional email address, and a password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form and we cannot read them.
  • When you place an order: your name, phone number, hostel/hall or delivery location, class name, an optional index number, and the items you ordered.
  • When you sell: your listings (titles, descriptions, prices, stock, photos) and the details you gave when you applied to become a seller.
  • On your device: your sign-in session and your shopping cart are saved in your browser so the site works between visits. We also count how many people view or tap an advertisement on the homepage; this does not identify you.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To process orders and let you track them.
  • To let sellers contact the customers who ordered their items, and to let buyers contact sellers.
  • To send transactional text messages (SMS), such as an alert to a seller about a new order.
  • To review seller applications, prevent fraud and misuse, and help you if you need support or a password reset.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Everyone: a seller's name, their listings and photos, and the WhatsApp contact on those listings.
  • The seller of an item you order: your name, phone number, delivery location and class, so they can meet you and hand over the item.
  • ShopSphere moderators: account details, listings and orders, so they can approve sellers and resolve problems.
  • Service providers that help us run the site: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage), Arkesel (text messages) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). WhatsApp is used only when you choose to message someone.

We may also share information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to handle orders, disputes and legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time (see below).

How we protect it

Access is limited by account type, passwords are stored hashed, and the site is served over HTTPS. No online service is completely secure, so please choose a strong password, do not share it, and use Change password in the menu if you think someone else knows it.

Your choices

You may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may object to how we use it. If you forget your password, use Forgot password? on the sign-in form: a moderator will confirm your identity before giving you a temporary password.
