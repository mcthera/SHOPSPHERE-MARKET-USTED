Terms of Use & Community Safety Guidelines

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Operator: ARKTECH COMPANY (NyansaTech)

1. Overview & Community Vision

ShopSphere Market is a peer-to-peer campus marketplace designed exclusively for university students to buy, sell, and trade goods locally. Transactions rely on direct student-to-student communication via WhatsApp and safe in-person campus meetups. All payments must be made only to the official ShopSphere management account shown at checkout; do not pay sellers directly.

2. Seller Rules & Responsibilities

2.1 Verification & Listing Approval

  • Eligibility: Only verified student sellers or campus-approved vendors may register as sellers on ShopSphere.
  • Moderation Review: All seller submissions, product updates, and storefront details are subject to mandatory manual review and approval by ARKTECH COMPANY before being published.
  • Accurate Descriptions: Sellers must provide truthful, clear, and high-quality details and images.

2.2 Prohibited Goods & Services

  • Counterfeit, pirated, or stolen goods.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vaping devices, or illegal substances.
  • Firearms, weapons, fireworks, or hazardous chemicals.
  • Prescription medicines, health supplements, or medical devices.
  • Academic dishonesty materials (leakages, assignment writing services).
  • Adult content, explicit media, or offensive materials.

3. Buyer Guidelines & Responsibilities

  • Order Commitment: Placing an order generates a reference code and redirects you to WhatsApp. Only place orders you genuinely intend to purchase.
  • Official Payment Account: Make payments only to the ShopSphere management account shown at checkout: 0593263101, account name Mensah Peter Kofi. Never transfer or pay money directly to an individual seller.
  • No Ghosting: Submitting fake orders or ghosting meetups will result in immediate account suspension.

4. Safety Protocols for Campus Meetups

  • Public Places Only: Always meet in high-traffic, well-lit public campus locations (e.g., University Library forecourts, Student Union buildings, dining halls). NEVER meet in private hostel rooms or secluded areas.
  • Bring a Friend: Whenever possible, accompany a friend to the handover.
  • No Advance Payments: Never transfer funds via MoMo in advance before physical inspection.

5. Three-Strikes Policy & Moderation

  • 1st Violation: Formal warning and removal of listing.
  • 2nd Violation: 14 to 30 day suspension.
  • 3rd Violation: Permanent ban of account, phone number, and student profile.
